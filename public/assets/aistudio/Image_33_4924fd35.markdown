## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Claymorphism** and soft modern glassmorphism into a tactile, welcoming, and high-trust experience tailored for enterprise brand intelligence and AI-driven compliance.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Built around an unmistakable glowing 3D shield emblem, projecting institutional-grade protection, consistency, and accuracy.
- **Approachable & Delightful:** Soft, inflated 3D pill and card surfaces remove the rigid sterility often found in regulatory software, making continuous content auditing smooth and engaging.
- **Forward-Thinking & Intelligent:** Vibrant periwinkle, royal blue, and lavender gradients paired with illuminated specular highlights convey cutting-edge AI agency.

### Visual Aesthetic & Design Movement
- **Tactile Claymorphism:** Thick, cushion-like cards and navigation pills detailed with double-layered inner highlights (inset top/left specular white) and deep, diffused ambient drop shadows underneath.
- **Floating Spatial Depth:** Multi-tiered floating containers set over ethereal, atmospheric pastel spheres in light mode, and deep cosmic violet-indigo vacuums in dark mode.
- **High Clarity SaaS Foundation:** Retains crisp typography, strict hierarchical metric grids, and unambiguous operational status signifiers.

## Colors

The color system delivers high aesthetic resonance in both Light and Dark paradigms, employing luminous periwinkle-blue primaries, lavender accents, and high-contrast semantic badges.

### Light Mode Foundations
- **Canvas Gradient:** `#F2F4FD` blending smoothly into `#E9EDFC` with atmospheric floating ambient spheres.
- **Card Surfaces:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` with 85%–95% opacity, topped with crisp inset white highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`).
- **Primary Actions:** Electric royal blue-to-violet linear gradients (`#4F46E5` → `#6366F1`).
- **Typography:** Deep twilight navy `#0F172A` for primary headers, `#475569` for body descriptions, and `#64748B` for secondary labels.

### Dark Mode Foundations
- **Canvas Deep Tone:** Rich obsidian-violet `#070614` to midnight indigo `#0D0B24`.
- **Containers & Glass Panels:** Deep translucent navy `#13112E` at 70% opacity, bordered by glowing periwinkle specular borders (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`).
- **Glow & Lighting Accents:** Neon periwinkle `#818CF8` and ultraviolet `#A855F7`.

### Semantic Color Coding
- **Approved / Compliant:** Mint jade green `#10B981` (background: `#ECFDF5`, text: `#065F46`).
- **Pending Review / Needs Attention:** Warm soft amber `#F59E0B` (background: `#FFFBEB`, text: `#92400E`).
- **Needs Review / Issues Found / Rejected:** Soft crimson coral `#EF4444` (background: `#FEF2F2`, text: `#991B1B`).

## Typography

Typography is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric foundation, open counters, and contemporary SaaS friendliness. It provides optimal readability in dense tabular reports and data cards, while feeling sculpted and polished in bold clay headlines.

### Hierarchical Guidelines
- **Hero Headers & Page Titles:** Rendered with `headline-xl` (32px, 800 weight) in deep midnight navy (`#0F172A`) or bright crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) against banner glass.
- **Metric Totals & KPI Values:** Use bold display figures with tight negative tracking (`-0.03em`) for instant scanning.
- **Body & Explanatory Copy:** `body-md` (14px) provides clean legibility for rule definitions and suggestions.
- **Badges & Labels:** `label-md` and `label-sm` use semi-bold and bold weights with subtle uppercase tracking to distinguish statuses inside rounded capsules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a structured fluid grid optimized for expansive dashboard workstations and adaptable SaaS responsive views.

### Structure & Grids
- **Sidebar Navigation:** Fixed-width pill container on desktop (260px–280px) floating with external padding from the viewport edges. On tablet/mobile, collapses to a floating horizontal bar or off-canvas drawer.
- **Main Canvas:** Fluid multi-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and outer canvas margins of 32px (`2rem`).
- **Dashboard Banner:** Spans full content width with an integrated 3D shield presentation and contextual greeting actions.
- **Modular Card Grids:** 4-column metric arrangement on desktop, reflowing to 2 columns on tablet, and single stacked cards on mobile devices.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Outer margin scales down to `1rem`. Banner collapses into a stacked vertical presentation. Action tables shift to card views.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar converts to an icon-rail format (80px width) or off-canvas overlay.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Full 12-column arrangement with dual-card side-by-side verification (e.g., Original Content vs. Suggested Version).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **multi-layered claymorphic illumination** rather than flat dark shadows.

### Light Mode Clay Elevation
- **Base Cards & Floating Containers:**
  - *Box Shadow:* `0 20px 35px -8px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.18)`
  - *Specular Edge (Inner Highlight):* `inset 0 2px 3px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95), inset 0 -2px 4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.04)`
  - *Border:* `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`
- **Inflated Clay Buttons (Primary):**
  - *Gradient Fill:* Linear gradient (`135deg, #6366F1 0%, #4F46E5 100%`)
  - *Inner Clay Volume:* `inset 0 3px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4), inset 0 -3px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`
  - *Drop Glow:* `0 10px 20px -3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.4)`

### Dark Mode Luminous Elevation
- **Deep Containers:**
  - *Surface:* `rgba(19, 17, 46, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`
  - *Border:* `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`
  - *Outer Aura:* `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 25px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`
  - *Specular Inset:* `inset 0 1.5px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25), inset 0 -2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`
- **Interactive Focus & Glowing Badges:** Peripheral ambient glows matching the semantic color (e.g., green aura for Approved badges, purple aura for active navigation pills).

## Shapes

The shape system embraces hyper-rounded, cushioned geometry that defines the clay aesthetic.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Defined with generous `24px` to `32px` corner radii, generating a smooth, ballooned feel.
- **Interactive Pills:** Full pill contouring (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) used for active navigation indicators, status badges, secondary buttons, and input search capsules.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces:** Form fields, document drop zones, and comparison panels maintain proportional inner corner radii between `16px` and `20px` to mirror the outer card silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action Buttons:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with vibrant violet-blue gradients, white semi-bold text, inset top gloss (`inset 0 2px 2px rgba(255,255,255,0.45)`), and dynamic bottom elevation shadows.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons:** Translucent white background (or dark indigo glass in dark mode), subtle `1px` periwinkle border, and smooth scale transitions on hover (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Navigation Menu Pills:** Lateral nav items sit inside continuous sidebar rails. The active route transforms into an inflated gradient pill with illuminated white typography and drop shadows.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Semantic Capsule Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `6px 14px`.
  - **Approved:** Emerald green circle dot or check icon, soft mint fill, crisp dark green typography.
  - **Pending Review:** Warm amber clock icon, soft honey background, amber text.
  - **Needs Review / Issues:** Soft coral-red warning icon, light crimson background, deep red text.

### Metric KPI & Data Cards
- Inflated square and rectangular cards featuring an icon enclosure (pill or squircle with matching tint), prominent bold metric values (e.g., "12 Total Checks"), and trend badge tags with upward or downward indicators (`+12%`).

### Comparison Panels & Diff Viewers
- Dual clay panels placed side-by-side: "Original Content" with neutral outline and subtle dimmed text, an animated directional transition pill (`→`), and "Suggested Version" highlighted with vivid mint or periwinkle emphasis on corrected tokens. Includes a floating 1-click "Copy" pill button.

### File Upload & Drop Zones
- Cushioned container with dashed periwinkle guide border, centered inflated 3D cloud/upload icon button, supported format chips, and clay action triggers ("Choose File" / "Paste Content").

### Global Header & Search Pill
- Integrated top navigation bar housing a full-pill search capsule with shortcut tag (`⌘ + K`), clay-styled notification bell with indicator dot, user profile chip with chevron dropdown, and an illuminated light/dark clay slider toggle.
